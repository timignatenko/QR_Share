How a transfer works

Four steps, no accounts, no waiting room.

1

Open a room

Start sending and a QR code appears instantly, along with a link you can share instead.

2

Share the code

Let the other device scan it with a camera, or paste the copied link directly.

3

Wait for the handshake

The moment the two devices meet, the status turns green — no delay, no approval step.

4

Drop your files

Drag files into the room and they stream straight to the other device as they go.

QR Share — files travel peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Nothing is uploaded, cached, or logged.
